# Privacy Policy — Howler

_Last updated: [EFFECTIVE DATE]_

Howler ("the app") is a sound level (SPL) meter developed by Lorenzo Ciacci
("House of Yeti", "we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app does and
does not handle. The short version: Howler does its work entirely on your
device and collects nothing.

## Data we collect

**None.** Howler does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It has
no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking or
data-collection SDKs.

## Microphone use

Howler requires microphone access (`RECORD_AUDIO`) for one purpose: to measure
the sound pressure level of your surroundings in real time. Audio from the
microphone is processed on your device, in memory, as it arrives, to compute a
decibel reading. 

**Your audio is never recorded, saved, or transmitted.** No audio data leaves
your device, and Howler does not retain any recording of what your microphone
hears.

## Data sharing

We do not share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any.

## Calibration settings

If you set a manual calibration value, it is stored locally on your device so it
persists between sessions. It is not personal data and is never transmitted.

## Children's privacy

Howler is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children. It
collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change in the future (for example, if optional
crash reporting is ever added), we will update this policy and the app's Google
Play Data Safety disclosures before that change takes effect.
